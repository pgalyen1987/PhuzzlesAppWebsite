Phuzzles A Rebel Studios app

Put the daily puzzle on your site

A new photo puzzle every day, solved on your page without anyone leaving it. The photos are public domain, which is the usual reason a site cannot host someone else’s picture, and the credit sits in the note under each one.

The line you paste

<iframe src="https://phuzzles.app/embed/"
        width="100%" height="560" style="border:0"
        title="Phuzzle of the day" loading="lazy"></iframe>

What you get

  • A new puzzle every day at 11:00 Eastern. It changes itself; there is nothing to update.
  • Every photo is public domain or CC0, and the note under it names the source. Nothing you host here belongs to anyone who can ask for it back.
  • No analytics, no cookies, nothing stored on your readers. The puzzle keeps a half-finished board in their own browser and that is all.
  • Free, with no account and no key.

What it will not do

  • It needs JavaScript, and it fetches the day’s puzzle from Firebase. On a page without either, the frame stays empty.
  • It does not resize itself. 560 px of height suits the 4×4 board; give it more if your column is wide.
  • You cannot choose the photo, the difficulty or the language. It is one puzzle a day, the same one everybody gets.
  • There is no API and no theming. If you need either, write and say what for.

The catch

There isn’t a hidden one. The widget carries our name under it and two links — one to make a puzzle of your own, one to the day’s puzzle on our site. That is what we get out of it, and it is the whole of what we get out of it. If it stops being worth that to you, delete the line.
