Phuzzles Child Safety
Child Safety Standards

Protecting every user

Phuzzles has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This page describes our standards, prohibited content, and how to report violations.

Effective: January 1, 2025
Our Commitment

Zero-tolerance policy

Rebel Studios, the developer of Phuzzles, is unequivocally opposed to child sexual abuse and exploitation in any form. We do not allow, facilitate, or tolerate content, behaviour, or communication that sexualises, exploits, or endangers minors anywhere on our platform.

Any account found to have engaged in CSAE will be permanently banned immediately, reported to the appropriate authorities, and reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required by US law.

Prohibited Content & Behaviour

What is strictly prohibited

The following are absolutely forbidden on Phuzzles under all circumstances:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including images, video, or illustrated content
  • Sending, receiving, or storing sexually explicit content involving a minor
  • Grooming behaviour — using the app to build trust with a minor for the purpose of exploitation
  • Soliciting sexual content or contact from anyone under the age of 18
  • Sharing personal information (address, school, location) of a minor without parental consent
  • Any communication intended to facilitate physical access to a child for exploitative purposes
Platform Safeguards

How we protect users

Phuzzles is a photo puzzle messaging app. We take the following steps to keep all users safe:

  • Users can block any contact at any time, immediately preventing further communication
  • Users can report any contact or received puzzle directly within the app
  • All reports are reviewed and accounts engaging in prohibited behaviour are permanently banned
  • Puzzle images are stored in Firebase Storage with access controlled by authenticated user rules — content is not publicly browsable
  • We do not knowingly permit children under 13 to create accounts, consistent with COPPA requirements
  • Account deletion permanently removes all associated content from our systems
Reporting

How to report a violation

If you encounter content or behaviour on Phuzzles that you believe involves the sexual exploitation of a child, report it immediately using one of the methods below.

Report within the app

Open the app → go to the relevant contact or puzzle → tap the ⋮ menu → select "Report User". Your report will be reviewed promptly.

Email our safety team

For urgent or sensitive reports, contact us directly:

safety@phuzzles.app

🔒 Required reporting: Rebel Studios is legally required under 18 U.S.C. § 2258A to report any apparent CSAM to the NCMEC CyberTipline . We comply fully with this obligation. You can also file a report directly at missingkids.org/cybertipline or contact the FBI or your local law enforcement.

Questions about this policy

If you have questions about our child safety standards or want to understand how we handle a specific situation, you can reach our team at:
