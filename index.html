Phuzzles A Rebel Studios app

Send a photo they have to solve.

Phuzzles cuts your photo into pieces and shuffles them. Your friend swaps them back, and the message you wrote only shows up when the last piece lands.

Free, with ads.

On the box

What you get, and what happens to your photo.

Pieces
9, 16, 25 or 36. Easy is 3×3, Expert is 6×6.
Message
Up to 200 characters. It stays hidden until the puzzle is solved.
Sending
To someone who has Phuzzles too. Find them by username in the app, or text them an invite first.
Solving
In the app. A link to a public puzzle also opens in any browser, no install needed.
Daily
A new puzzle every day at 11:00 Eastern, made from a public-domain photo. Solve today's in your browser: phuzzles.app/today
Kept for
30 days. After that the puzzle and its photo are deleted from our servers.
Price
Free, with ads. There's no paid version yet.

Phuzzles is made by Rebel Studios. Questions, bugs or a puzzle that won't open: rebelstudiossoftware@gmail.com
