Phuzzles A Rebel Studios app

Send a photo they have to solve.

Send it as a link in any chat. They solve it in their browser, with nothing to install.

Phuzzles cuts your photo into pieces and shuffles them. Your friend swaps them back, and the message you wrote only shows up when the last piece lands.

Free, with ads.

On the box

What you get, and what happens to your photo.

Pieces
9, 16, 25 or 36. Easy is 3×3, Expert is 6×6.
Message
Up to 200 characters. It stays hidden until the puzzle is solved.
Sending
To someone who has Phuzzles, by username, or to a group. You can also send any puzzle as a link, to anyone.
Solving
In the app, or from a link in any phone's browser, no install needed.
Daily
A new puzzle every day at 11:00 Eastern, made from a public-domain photo. Solve today's in your browser: phuzzles.app/today
Price
Free, with ads. Phuzzles Plus is US$2.99 a month: no ads, and the Feed without a daily limit.

Phuzzles is made by Rebel Studios. Questions, bugs or a puzzle that won't open: support@phuzzles.app
