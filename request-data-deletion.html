Send us an email

Email us from the address associated with your Phuzzles account and include the following so we can locate it:

  • The email address on your account
  • Your Phuzzles username (if you remember it)
  • Subject line: Data Deletion Request
Email support@phuzzles.app

Or copy the address: rebelstudiossoftware@gmail.com

What happens next

  • We verify your request We confirm the email matches a registered Phuzzles account.
  • Data permanently deleted Your account, profile, puzzles, contacts, and images are removed from Firebase.
  • We reply to confirm You'll get an email once deletion is complete. The whole process takes up to 30 days.
