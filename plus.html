Phuzzles Plus

More puzzles,
no ads.

Puzzles a friend sends you are unlimited, with Plus or without it, and they always will be. Plus is for the public feed — the puzzles strangers have shared, where a free account can start three a day.

What you get

  • Every puzzle in the feed, as many as you like, instead of three a day.
  • No ads, anywhere in the app.
  • A daily pack — solve the picture, then read what it actually is.
  • Puzzles from friends stay unlimited either way. Plus does not change them.

Browsing the feed, sending puzzles and the daily puzzle are free and are not metered. What Plus buys is how many feed puzzles you may start in a day.

What it costs

US$2.99 a month

It renews every month until you cancel, and the app shows the price in your own currency before you pay. You can cancel any time, in the App Store or on Google Play — Plus then keeps working until the month you have paid for runs out.

What it does not do

  • There is no free trial. The first month is the full price.
  • It belongs to the store you bought it from. Buying it on an iPhone does not unlock it on an Android phone, because Plus is not attached to your Phuzzles account.
  • Deleting your Phuzzles account does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it in the store first, or Apple or Google will keep charging you.
  • It does not add puzzle sizes, jigsaw mode or streaks. Those are in the free app.

The full terms, including refunds, are in section 6 of the Terms.

Get the app

Plus is bought inside the app, on the Plus screen. The app is free.
