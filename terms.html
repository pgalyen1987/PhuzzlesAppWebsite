6. Phuzzles Plus (paid subscription)

Phuzzles is free to use. Phuzzles Plus is an optional subscription that renews automatically every month until you cancel it. You do not need it to send a puzzle to someone or to solve one they sent you.

What it costs

US$2.99 per month. Outside the United States the price is the local equivalent set by the store, and the exact amount is shown on the purchase screen before you confirm. There is no free trial: the first month is charged when you subscribe.

What it includes

  • Every puzzle in the public feed, with no daily limit. A free account can start 3 feed puzzles a day.
  • No ads, anywhere in the app.
  • A daily pack of puzzles.

Puzzles sent to you by someone you know are unlimited either way, with Plus or without it. Plus changes how much of the public feed you can play, not what you can do with your friends.

Billing, renewal and cancelling

Payment is taken by the store you bought from, not by us. It is charged to your Apple Account or your Google Play account when you confirm the purchase, and again each month within 24 hours before the current month ends, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before that. Cancelling stops the next charge; Plus keeps working until the month you have already paid for runs out, and part-months are not refunded.

You can see, change or cancel the subscription at any time in your store account: App Store subscriptions on iPhone, or Google Play subscriptions on Android. Deleting your Phuzzles account does not cancel the subscription, because we cannot cancel it for you: do that in the store first. Refunds are decided by the store that took the payment, under its own policy, not by us. If the price ever changes, the store asks you to agree to the new price before it applies.

One limit worth knowing before you pay

Plus is tied to the store you bought it from. Buying it on an iPhone unlocks it for that Apple Account, and buying it on Android unlocks it for that Google account. If you use both an iPhone and an Android phone, one subscription will not cover both of them, because we do not yet attach Plus to your Phuzzles account. On a new phone with the same store account, use Already subscribed? Restore on the Plus screen.
